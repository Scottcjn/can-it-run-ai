Weird Hardware Hall of Fame — WANTED

Unclaimed firsts. Run real inference on one of these, submit proof, and your name goes here permanently. Bragging rights are the reward; they do not depreciate.

The Rules

  • Real hardware or honestly labeled emulation. An emulated run presented as real gets pulled.
  • Proof required. Uncut boot-to-output video, or raw logs + sha256 checksums of binary and model. Both is better.
  • Honest limitations section required. Say what the demo does not show.
  • Corrections welcome. Disputed entries get marked disputed, not buried.
  • Failed attempts count. Negative results are data.

Verification methodology borrows from RustChain's Proof-of-Antiquity hardware fingerprinting — timing behavior, cache characteristics, honest self-reporting.
